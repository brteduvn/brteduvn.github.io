# Privacy / Quyền riêng tư

EduPlan OSS v0.1.0 does not require an account, telemetry, cloud storage or AI key. Source documents are read locally by the browser; exported documents are downloaded to the device. Data remains in memory until the page is refreshed. We do not operate a document upload endpoint. Beware that hosting providers may record standard access logs; extensions and device software may inspect content. Do not upload personal or confidential student records to third-party issue trackers.
