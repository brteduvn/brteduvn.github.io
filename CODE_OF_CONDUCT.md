# Code of Conduct / Quy tắc ứng xử

We aim to build an inclusive community of educators and open-source contributors.

### Expected
Be respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, accept technical critique, use accessible language, and protect the dignity and privacy of students, teachers and maintainers.

### Unacceptable
Harassment, threats, bullying, discrimination, unwanted personal contact, publishing private information, abusive language, and deliberately misleading reports or contribution metrics.

### Enforcement
Maintainers may remove harmful content and limit participation to protect contributors. Report incidents privately to **admin@brt.edu.vn**, not in public issues when personal information is involved. Reports will be handled with discretion and proportionate action.

This policy applies to project repositories, PRs, issues and other official community spaces.
